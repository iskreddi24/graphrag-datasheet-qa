- Primary Deployment Target Verticals: Long-Range IoT Telemetry, Smart Agriculture, Remote Infrastructure Sensing, Disaster Warning

## 6. Critical Silicon Errata & Engineering Guidelines
High transmit power output (+22 dBm) causes substantial peak current consumption (up to 118 mA). Must implement low-ESR bulk capacitance on VBAT_RF and ensure antenna matching network complies with ETSI/FCC harmonics limits.
