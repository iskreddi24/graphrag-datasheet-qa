# Semiconductor Technical Datasheet: ESP32-S3-WROOM-1

## 1. Product Overview & Identity
- Part Identifier: ESP32-S3-WROOM-1
- Product Title: Espressif ESP32-S3 Dual-Core AI SoC
- Semiconductor Classification: Wireless SoC
- Original Manufacturer: Espressif Systems
- Physical Packaging: QFN-56 (7x7mm)
- Thermal Operating Specification: -40°C to +85°C

## 2. Processing Core & Architectural Profile
- Core Architecture: Dual-core 32-bit Xtensa LX7
- Peak Operating Clock: 240 MHz
- Integrated Non-Volatile Flash: 8192 KB
- High-Speed Static RAM (SRAM): 512 KB

## 3. Electrical & Power Ratings
- Minimum Recommended Supply Voltage: 3.0 V
- Maximum Recommended Supply Voltage: 3.6 V
- Typical Active Operating Current: 68.0 mA (at nominal clock)
- Low-Power Sleep / Leakage Current: 5.0 uA (deep standby)

## 4. Hardware Interfaces & Peripheral Buses
- Supported Protocols and Busses: Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, BLE 5.0, SPI, I2C, UART, USB-OTG

## 5. System Interoperability & Companion ICs
- Verified Compatible Companion Devices: BME688-MEMS, ATECC608A-TNGTLS
- Primary Deployment Target Verticals: Edge AI Vision & Voice, Industrial IoT, Smart Home Gateway

## 6. Critical Silicon Errata & Engineering Guidelines
GPIO34 through GPIO39 are input-only and lack internal weak pull-up/pull-down resistors. Dedicated decoupling capacitors (10uF + 0.1uF) required close to VDD33 pins to prevent brownout during Wi-Fi calibration bursts.
