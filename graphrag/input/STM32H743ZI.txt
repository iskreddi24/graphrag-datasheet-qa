# Semiconductor Technical Datasheet: STM32H743ZI

## 1. Product Overview & Identity
- Part Identifier: STM32H743ZI
- Product Title: STMicroelectronics STM32H7 High-Performance MCU
- Semiconductor Classification: Microcontroller
- Original Manufacturer: STMicroelectronics
- Physical Packaging: LQFP-144 (20x20mm)
- Thermal Operating Specification: -40°C to +85°C

## 2. Processing Core & Architectural Profile
- Core Architecture: 32-bit Arm Cortex-M7 with DP-FPU
- Peak Operating Clock: 480 MHz
- Integrated Non-Volatile Flash: 2048 KB
- High-Speed Static RAM (SRAM): 1024 KB

## 3. Electrical & Power Ratings
- Minimum Recommended Supply Voltage: 1.71 V
- Maximum Recommended Supply Voltage: 3.6 V
- Typical Active Operating Current: 220.0 mA (at nominal clock)
- Low-Power Sleep / Leakage Current: 2.8 uA (deep standby)

## 4. Hardware Interfaces & Peripheral Buses
- Supported Protocols and Busses: CAN-FD, SPI, I2C, UART, Ethernet 10/100, USB-OTG, SAI

## 5. System Interoperability & Companion ICs
- Verified Compatible Companion Devices: SX1262-LORA, ATECC608A-TNGTLS
- Primary Deployment Target Verticals: Industrial Automation, Motor Control, High-End Audio, Avionics Telemetry

## 6. Critical Silicon Errata & Engineering Guidelines
Errata ES0392: AXI SRAM concurrent DMA reads during high-speed burst transfers may cause clock synchronization stalls unless AHB priority arbitration is configured. External SMPS power supply requires 4.7uH inductor.
