# Semiconductor Technical Datasheet: nRF52840

## 1. Product Overview & Identity
- Part Identifier: nRF52840
- Product Title: Nordic Semiconductor Multi-Protocol Bluetooth 5.3 SoC
- Semiconductor Classification: Wireless SoC
- Original Manufacturer: Nordic Semiconductor
- Physical Packaging: aQFN-73 (7x7mm)
- Thermal Operating Specification: -40°C to +105°C

## 2. Processing Core & Architectural Profile
- Core Architecture: 32-bit Arm Cortex-M4F with FPU
- Peak Operating Clock: 64 MHz
- Integrated Non-Volatile Flash: 1024 KB
- High-Speed Static RAM (SRAM): 256 KB

## 3. Electrical & Power Ratings
- Minimum Recommended Supply Voltage: 1.7 V
- Maximum Recommended Supply Voltage: 5.5 V
- Typical Active Operating Current: 4.8 mA (at nominal clock)
- Low-Power Sleep / Leakage Current: 0.4 uA (deep standby)

## 4. Hardware Interfaces & Peripheral Buses
- Supported Protocols and Busses: BLE 5.3, Thread, Zigbee, NFC, USB 2.0, SPI, I2C, UART

## 5. System Interoperability & Companion ICs
- Verified Compatible Companion Devices: BME688-MEMS, ATECC608A-TNGTLS, SX1262-LORA
- Primary Deployment Target Verticals: Medical Wearables, Connected Health, Asset Tracking, Smart Lighting

## 6. Critical Silicon Errata & Engineering Guidelines
Errata PAN-214: High DC-DC converter regulator ripple during high transmit power (+8 dBm) requires strict PCB ground stitching around C16/C17 matching network. NFC antenna pins NFC1/NFC2 default to NFC mode; reconfigure in UICR for standard GPIO usage.
