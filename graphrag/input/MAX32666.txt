# Semiconductor Technical Datasheet: MAX32666

## 1. Product Overview & Identity
- Part Identifier: MAX32666
- Product Title: Analog Devices / Maxim Low-Power Dual-Core BLE MCU
- Semiconductor Classification: Wireless SoC
- Original Manufacturer: Analog Devices
- Physical Packaging: WLP-108 (4.4x4.4mm)
- Thermal Operating Specification: -40°C to +105°C

## 2. Processing Core & Architectural Profile
- Core Architecture: Dual-core Arm Cortex-M4F with FPU + BLE 5.2
- Peak Operating Clock: 96 MHz
- Integrated Non-Volatile Flash: 1024 KB
- High-Speed Static RAM (SRAM): 560 KB

## 3. Electrical & Power Ratings
- Minimum Recommended Supply Voltage: 1.71 V
- Maximum Recommended Supply Voltage: 3.6 V
- Typical Active Operating Current: 8.5 mA (at nominal clock)
- Low-Power Sleep / Leakage Current: 1.5 uA (deep standby)

## 4. Hardware Interfaces & Peripheral Buses
- Supported Protocols and Busses: BLE 5.2, SPI, I2C, UART, 1-Wire, USB-FS

## 5. System Interoperability & Companion ICs
- Verified Compatible Companion Devices: BME688-MEMS, ATECC608A-TNGTLS
- Primary Deployment Target Verticals: Clinical Patient Monitoring, Wearable Biosensing, Smart Hearables, Sports Telemetry

## 6. Critical Silicon Errata & Engineering Guidelines
SIMO buck regulator output voltages must be sequenced in strict order (VREG1 -> VREG2) during cold power-on. Wafer-Level Packaging (WLP) requires underfill epoxy to mitigate solder fatigue in thermal cycling.
