# Semiconductor Technical Datasheet: STM32U585

## 1. Product Overview & Identity
- Part Identifier: STM32U585
- Product Title: STMicroelectronics Ultra-Low-Power Cortex-M33 MCU
- Semiconductor Classification: Microcontroller
- Original Manufacturer: STMicroelectronics
- Physical Packaging: LQFP-100 (14x14mm)
- Thermal Operating Specification: -40°C to +85°C

## 2. Processing Core & Architectural Profile
- Core Architecture: 32-bit Arm Cortex-M33 with TrustZone
- Peak Operating Clock: 160 MHz
- Integrated Non-Volatile Flash: 2048 KB
- High-Speed Static RAM (SRAM): 786 KB

## 3. Electrical & Power Ratings
- Minimum Recommended Supply Voltage: 1.71 V
- Maximum Recommended Supply Voltage: 3.6 V
- Typical Active Operating Current: 19.0 mA (at nominal clock)
- Low-Power Sleep / Leakage Current: 1.2 uA (deep standby)

## 4. Hardware Interfaces & Peripheral Buses
- Supported Protocols and Busses: CAN-FD, USB-C FS, SPI, I2C, UART, SAI

## 5. System Interoperability & Companion ICs
- Verified Compatible Companion Devices: BME688-MEMS, ATECC608A-TNGTLS
- Primary Deployment Target Verticals: Battery-Powered Industrial IoT, Smart Energy Meters, Portable Medical Devices

## 6. Critical Silicon Errata & Engineering Guidelines
Low-power autonomous peripherals (LPBAM) require background SRAM4 retention voltage enabled in PWR register before entering Stop 3 sleep state. Ensure USB Type-C CC pins have pull-down terminations.
